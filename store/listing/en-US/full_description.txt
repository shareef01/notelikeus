Notelikeus is a fast, private notes app inspired by Google Keep.

On Android, your notes live on your device in an encrypted database. Google sign-in is optional — turn on cloud sync when you want the same notes on web and other devices.

CAPTURE IDEAS YOUR WAY
• Write notes with titles, colors, and rich text (bold, italic, links)
• Build checklists and organize with labels
• Pin important notes
• Switch themes (light, dark, OLED, and more)

STAY ON TOP OF THINGS
• Search across titles, body text, checklists, and labels
• Archive and trash with undo
• Set date/time reminders with notifications
• Reorder notes in list view
• List, grid, and compact layouts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Encrypted local database (SQLCipher)
• Optional biometric lock for the whole app
• Notes sync only to your own Firebase account
• No ads, no analytics

CLOUD SYNC (OPTIONAL ON ANDROID)
• Sign in with Google to sync note text via Firebase
• Auto-sync when signed in, or sync manually from Settings
• Works on Firebase free (Spark) tier — no paid plan required for text sync
• Use the web app at notelike.web.app when signed in

BACKUP & WIDGET
• Export and import notes as JSON anytime
• Home screen widget for quick access

Notelikeus works fully offline on Android. Cloud sync is optional and can be turned on or off in Settings.
