Notelikeus is a fast, private notes app inspired by Google Keep.

Your notes live on your device in an encrypted database. Google sign-in is optional — turn on cloud sync when you want the same notes on the web app and your other devices.

CAPTURE IDEAS YOUR WAY
• Write notes with titles, colors, and rich text (bold, italic, links)
• Build checklists and organize with labels
• Attach images to a note
• Pin important notes
• Switch themes (light, dark, pure black, and accent colors)

STAY ON TOP OF THINGS
• Search across titles, body text, checklists, and labels
• Archive and trash with undo
• Set date/time reminders with notifications
• Reorder notes in list view
• List, grid, and compact layouts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Encrypted local database (SQLCipher)
• Optional biometric lock for the whole app
• Cloud notes are isolated to your own account
• No ads, no analytics

CLOUD SYNC (OPTIONAL)
• Sign in with Google to sync notes across your devices
• Auto-sync when signed in, or sync manually from Settings
• Use the web app at notelikeus.pages.dev when signed in

BACKUP & WIDGET
• Export and import notes as JSON anytime
• Home screen widget for quick access

Notelikeus works fully offline. Cloud sync is optional and can be turned on or off in Settings.
